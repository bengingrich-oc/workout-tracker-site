Privacy policy

Workout Tracker · last updated October 16, 2026 · Home

Workout Tracker is a private app used by two people. This page explains what it stores and how it uses data from Google and Peloton.

What the app stores

  • Your account: email, name and, if you add them, a profile picture, height, weight goal and target date.
  • Your plan and history: workouts, how they felt, notes, food logs and weigh-ins.
  • If you link a partner: your partner sees your planned and finished workouts, your name and your picture. Your notes, food, weight and goals stay private.

Data is kept in the app's own database (Supabase), protected so each person can only reach their own data. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything but running the app.

Google sign-in and Google Calendar

  • Sign in with Google uses your Google email and name to create or open your account.
  • Calendar sync is optional. If you turn it on, the app can list your calendars (so you can pick one) and add, update and remove its own workout events there. It never reads, changes or deletes your other events.
  • Google's access key for your calendar is stored on the app's server, not on your phone, and is never shown to anyone. Turning calendar sync off removes the app's events from your calendar, revokes the app's access and deletes the key. You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Workout Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Peloton

Connecting Peloton is optional. You sign in on Peloton's own page; the app never sees your Peloton password. It keeps Peloton's access key on its server to list classes and see which ones you finished. Disconnecting deletes it.

Deleting your data

In the app, You → Start over removes your plan and history. To delete your whole account, email the address below.
