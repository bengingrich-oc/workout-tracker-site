Workout Tracker

Plan it. Do it. Together.

Workout Tracker is a private iPhone app for two people. It turns your goals, the activities you like, your gear and your free time into a weekly workout plan, then tracks workouts, food, weight and progress with your partner.

  • A weekly plan that fits around work and routine activities
  • Reminders, and missed workouts moved instead of piling up
  • Optional: matching Peloton classes, and your workouts on your Google Calendar

It's a personal project, not a public product. Privacy policy
